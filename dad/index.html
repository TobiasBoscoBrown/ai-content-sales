Hey Dad, let's get you a real business email.

Something like richard@yardbids.com instead of a Gmail address. It looks way more professional and it costs nothing. The whole thing takes about 5 minutes and you already own everything you need (the yardbids.com domain on Cloudflare).

FREE 5 minutes No new accounts

We're going to use a free Cloudflare feature called Email Routing. It takes any email sent to richard@yardbids.com and forwards it into your existing Gmail inbox. You read it, reply to it, and write new ones from a single place. Same Gmail you already use, just with a fancier address on the front.

What you'll need handy

  • Your Cloudflare login (the one tied to Rbrown@yardbids.com).
  • The Gmail address you want emails forwarded to (whichever inbox you actually check).
  • About 5 minutes.

Heads up: Cloudflare will send a one-click verification email to that Gmail to confirm it's really yours. Just keep that tab open.

1

Open Cloudflare Email Routing

Click the button below. If it asks you to log in, use your normal Cloudflare account.

Open Email Routing for yardbids.com →

If that link doesn't drop you exactly on the right page, no problem: log into Cloudflare → click yardbids.com → in the left sidebar click Email → Email Routing.

2

Turn on Email Routing (if it's not already on)

If you see a big Get started or Enable Email Routing button, click it. Cloudflare will offer to add the right DNS records for you automatically. Click "Add records and enable". That's it for this step.

If it says it's already enabled, even better, skip ahead.

3

Add your Gmail as a "Destination Address"

Look for the section called Destination addresses (sometimes under a "Routes" or "Settings" tab). Click Add destination address and type the Gmail you actually check.

Cloudflare will send a verification email to that Gmail. Open it. Click the Verify button. You'll get bounced back to Cloudflare and that destination will turn green / verified.

This step is the one most people get stuck on. The verification email is sometimes in Spam or Promotions, so peek there if you don't see it after 30 seconds.

4

Create your custom address

Now go to Custom addresses (or Routing rules) and click Create address.

Custom address
richard@yardbids.com
Pick whatever you like, richard@, contact@, hello@, your call.
Action
Send to an email
Then pick the Gmail you just verified.

Click Save. You're done. Send yourself a test from your phone to richard@yardbids.com and watch it land in your Gmail.

Optional but nice: send from richard@yardbids.com too

The setup above means you can receive mail at richard@yardbids.com. To also send as that address from inside Gmail:

  1. In Gmail click the gear icon → See all settings → Accounts and Import.
  2. Next to "Send mail as" click Add another email address.
  3. Name: Richard Brown. Email: richard@yardbids.com. Uncheck "Treat as alias" if you want it to look fully separate.
  4. For SMTP server, use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, your Gmail address, and a Google App Password (not your real Gmail password).
  5. Gmail will send you a verification code to richard@yardbids.com. It'll arrive in your normal Gmail inbox (because of the routing you just set up). Type the code in. Done.

If the App Password page asks for "App", pick Mail. If it asks for "Device", pick Other and type "yardbids". This is just so the password has a label.

If anything goes sideways

Just text or call me. Screenshot whatever Cloudflare is showing you and I'll walk you through it. The whole thing is reversible, you can't break anything.

Common gotchas:

  • Verification email never arrived → check Gmail's Spam and Promotions tabs.
  • "Domain not enabled" → step 2 wasn't finished, go back and click Enable.
  • "Destination not verified" → step 3 wasn't finished, click the Verify button in the Cloudflare email.
