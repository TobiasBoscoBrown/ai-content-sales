The goal: a real BuildMyTribe email at richard@buildmytribe.io that you can both receive and send from, all from inside your normal Gmail. No new inbox to check, no apps to install, no monthly cost. About 10 minutes total.

Two halves to this. Part 1 is on Cloudflare and gets you receiving mail at richard@buildmytribe.io. Part 2 is inside Gmail and lets you send from it too. Both are required for the full setup.

Heads up before you start

You already own the Cloudflare account that runs buildmytribe.io (it's the same login you use for yardbids.com). That means you have full admin rights on this, no permission walls, no waiting on me.

Have your Cloudflare login and your Gmail open in two tabs and you're set.

Part 1 · Receiving mail at richard@buildmytribe.io

1

Open Cloudflare Email Routing for buildmytribe.io

Click the button. If it asks you to log in, use your normal Cloudflare account.

Open Email Routing for buildmytribe.io →

If that link doesn't drop you exactly on the right page, no problem. Log into Cloudflare → click the buildmytribe.io domain → in the left sidebar click Email → Email Routing.

2

Add your Gmail as a "Destination Address"

Find the section called Destination addresses (it might be under a "Routes" tab). Click Add destination address and type the Gmail you actually check every day.

Cloudflare will send a verification email to that Gmail. Open it, click the big Verify button, and you'll get bounced back to Cloudflare. The destination flips to green.

If the verification email isn't there in 30 seconds, peek in Spam and Promotions. That's where it usually hides.

3

Create the richard@buildmytribe.io address

Go to Custom addresses (or Routing rules). Click Create address and fill it in:

Custom address
richard@buildmytribe.io
Action
Send to an email
Pick the Gmail you just verified.

Click Save. Quick test: from your phone, send a one-line email to richard@buildmytribe.io and watch it land in your Gmail. If it lands, Part 1 is done.

Part 2 · Sending from richard@buildmytribe.io inside Gmail

What this does

Right now you can receive at the new address. This part lets you click Compose in Gmail and pick richard@buildmytribe.io as the From line, so when you reply or write fresh emails, the recipient sees the BuildMyTribe address, not your personal Gmail.

1

Generate a Google App Password

Gmail won't let outside tools (or its own "Send mail as" feature) use your normal Gmail password. You need a one-time-use "App Password" instead. It's a 16-character code Google gives you.

Open Google App Passwords →
  • If it asks you to turn on 2-Step Verification first, do that. It's required.
  • Where it asks for an "app name", just type BuildMyTribe email.
  • Google will show you a 16-character password with spaces. Copy it. You won't see it again.
2

Open Gmail's "Send mail as" settings

In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings → Accounts and Import tab → next to "Send mail as", click Add another email address.

3

Fill the form like this

Name
Richard Brown

richard@buildmytribe.io

Uncheck "Treat as alias" if you want it to feel like a fully separate identity. Click Next Step.
